How to Use This PYQ

Use this previous year paper to understand the exam pattern and important topics.

  • Attempt the full paper under exam conditions — 3 hours, no notes
  • Identify weak topics and revise those first
  • Solve at least 3-4 years of PYQs for best preparation
  • Focus on questions that repeat across multiple years

💡 Pro Tip: 60-70% of DSEU SEM exam questions come from previous year papers. PYQs are the single most effective exam prep strategy.
